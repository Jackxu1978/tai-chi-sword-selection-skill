## 3. Four 60 Second Out of the Box Bench Checks

1. **Left Hand Reverse Salute Ear Check**: Tip lands cleanly between your earlobe and upper ear rim.
2. **Index Finger Fulcrum Check**: Bare sword balances around 2.4 to 3.5 in (approx. 6.0 to 9.0 cm) ahead of the cast alloy guard.
3. **Pommel Tap Full Tang Check**: Tapping the pommel produces one solid muted thud with zero metallic buzz or guard play.
4. **Controlled Distal Flex Check**: Lower two thirds remain firm while the front third bows smoothly around 15 to 25 degrees against carpet and springs back straight (never bend any Tai Chi blade tip to handle).
