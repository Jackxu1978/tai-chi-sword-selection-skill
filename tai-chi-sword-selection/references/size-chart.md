## 2. Reverse Salute Ear Tip Field Verification

Stand naturally with relaxed shoulders and hold the hilt vertically in your left hand reverse carry grip (*bei jian*) so the blade rests along the back of your vertical forearm with your arm hanging naturally at your side. On a properly matched 28, 30, or 32 inch tier, the rounded safety tip aligns cleanly between your earlobe and the top rim of your ear, guaranteeing floor clearance on low upward slices (*liao*) and tournament compliance where rules require the tip to reach at least the bottom of the earlobe.

## 3. Borderline Height, Low Ceiling, and Senior Sizing Rules

* **Borderline Height at 5 ft 3 in or 5 ft 8 in**: Size down one tier if you practice indoors under an 8 ft 0 in (244 cm) ceiling or have shorter arm proportion relative to torso height; choose the standard tier if you practice outdoors or have longer arms.
* **Seniors Over 70 and Wrist Sensitivity**: Size down one tier to the **28 in Petite (`ICETTCSF01`)** at around 1.30 lbs (approx. 590 g) bare weight and around 2.4 in (approx. 6.0 cm) guard balance point rather than switching to unbalanced wooden sticks.
* **Blade Length vs Overall Length**: Always compare steel blade length from guard face to tip. Generic marketplace listings advertising a 38 inch sword are quoting overall length on a 30 inch blade.
