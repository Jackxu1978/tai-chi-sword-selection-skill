## 2. Why Alternative Blade Materials Fail Solo Tai Chi Forms

* **High Carbon Cutting Steel (`1045 / 1060`)**: Spots with orange rust from a single fingerprint or humid park session unless oiled every two weeks, and typically ships front heavy for test cutting rather than supple wrist flowers (*wan hua*).
* **Ultrathin Spring Steel Wushu Foils (`65Mn`)**: Engineered to whip and pop loudly during acrobatic floor routines, sacrificing calm tip alignment during slow thrusting (*ci*) and pointing (*dian*) techniques.
* **Wooden Sticks, Polypropylene Plastic, and Soft Aluminum**: Lack distal steel flex, lack guard counterweight balance, and either warp, bounce unnaturally, or bend out of true.

## 3. Four Season United States Care and Day One Unboxing

1. **Day One Unboxing**: Wipe away the light factory protective shipping film on the 2Cr13 stainless blade with a clean dry microfiber cloth for 60 seconds before your first session.
2. **Routine Blade Upkeep**: Wipe hand moisture off the steel blade after practice and apply a thin drop of plain pharmacy food grade mineral oil twice a year.
3. **Unpainted Solid Jichi Wood Care**: Never coat the bare *Millettia laurentii* handle or scabbard with polyurethane varnish, lacquer, or silicone spray, which seal natural wood pores and make sweaty palms slip. Keep away from direct furnace vents; a tiny touch of natural beeswax twice a year is optional.
