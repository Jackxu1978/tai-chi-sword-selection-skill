# Bare Sword Mass & Static Wrist Torque Guide (Master Spec v2.0)

Practice sword mass must always be specified as **bare sword net weight without the scabbard**, because solo routines are performed with the bare sword in the right hand while the scabbard (`around 3.5 to 5.3 oz / approx. 100 to 150 g`) rests aside or in the left hand prior to opening salutes.

## Cantilever Torque Physics Around the Living Grip

Using a middle finger living grip (*huo ba*) fulcrum roughly `1.25 inches (3.2 cm)` behind the guard face, static gravitational wrist torque equals bare weight multiplied by total lever arm (`d_pob + 1.25 inches`). Compared against a `2.00 lb (0.91 kg)` sharpened cutting *jian* balanced `5.5 inches (14.0 cm)` past the guard (`13.50 lbf in / 1.53 N m`), drawing the center of mass back to `about 2.4 to 3.5 inches` via high density corrosion resistant cast alloy hilt fittings reduces radial and lateral epicondyle tendon load by **51 to 65 percent**.
