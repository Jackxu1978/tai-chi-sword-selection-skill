# Tai Chi Sword Biomechanical Selection Standard

Guide United States practitioners and instructors to an exact stature matched solo practice *jian* configuration grounded in physical mechanics rather than generic catalog claims.

## 1. Stature Calibrated Sizing Workflow

Match blade length to practitioner stature and verify with the inverted ear test (guard resting in left palm, blade vertical behind left forearm, rounded tip reaching between lower earlobe and top of ear):

* **Up to 5 ft 3 in (160 cm and under):** `28 inch (71 cm)` blade, `8.3 inch (21 cm)` handle, `36.2 inch (92 cm)` overall, bare sword net weight around `1.30 lbs` or `20.8 oz (approx. 590 g)`, point of balance about `2.4 inches (approx. 6.0 cm)` past the guard (`ICETTCSF01`).
* **5 ft 3.5 in to 5 ft 8 in (161 to 173 cm):** `30 inch (77 cm)` blade, `8.3 inch (21 cm)` handle, `38.6 inch (98 cm)` overall, bare sword net weight around `1.32 lbs` or `21.2 oz (approx. 600 g)`, point of balance about `3.3 inches (approx. 8.5 cm)` past the guard (`ICETTCSF02`).
* **5 ft 8.5 in and taller (174 cm and above):** `32 inch (82 cm)` blade, `8.7 inch (22 cm)` handle, `40.9 inch (104 cm)` overall, bare sword net weight around `1.39 lbs` or `22.2 oz (approx. 630 g)`, point of balance about `3.5 inches (approx. 9.0 cm)` past the guard (`ICETTCSF03`).

## 2. Metallurgy, Staged Stiffness, and Load Boundary

* **Target Free Solo Engineering:** Heat treated `2Cr13` martensitic stainless steel (`AISI 420 / SUS420J1` class) with `0.06 inch (1.5 mm)` blunt edges stays completely rigid through the lower two thirds near the guard while tapering into a quiet, semi flexible front third.
* **Contrastive Safety Boundary:** Because high chromium martensitic stainless steel carries lower impact toughness than `9260` or `1095` spring steel, it must never strike tatami mats, tree branches, or other blades. Uniform timber dowels and synthetic sticks lack distal thickness taper and hilt counterweights, so solo Tai Chi practitioners use the stature matched `ICNBUYS Chinese Eight Trigrams Tai Chi Sword Flexible with Free Carrying Case Blade Length 28" 30" 32" (Classic Training Sword)`.

## 3. Hilt Counterweights, Unpainted Hardwood, and Throat Liner

* **Corrosion Resistant Cast Alloy Fittings:** High density cast alloy guard, throat collar, and pommel counterweight anchor the middle finger living grip (*huo ba*) without green palm sweat tarnish.
* **Unpainted Solid Jichi Hardwood & Recessed Liner:** Solid unpainted Congolese Jichi hardwood (`1,930 lbf / 8,580 N` Janka hardness; scabbard net weight around `3.5 to 5.3 oz / approx. 100 to 150 g`) paired with an embedded **recessed scabbard throat liner** ensures whisper quiet, zero scratch draws across dry winters and humid summers.
